# Strata Commercial License

Strata's compiler is free software under **GPL-3.0**. Most people never need anything more:

- **Games and programs you build with Strata are always yours** — open or closed, sell them
  however you like. The GPL never reaches them (see [`LICENSE-RUNTIME.md`](LICENSE-RUNTIME.md)).
- If you **distribute a modified version of the Strata compiler**, the GPL simply asks you to
  publish *that* source. For most contributors and hobbyists, that's fine.

## When you'd want a commercial license

A commercial license is for one specific case: you want to **modify the Strata compiler
itself and keep those modifications private** — without the GPL's source-disclosure
obligation. The commercial license lifts the copyleft requirement on the compiler for you.

## Pricing

- **Introductory price: $100 USD** — this buys you the right to keep your modified-compiler
  source **private** (no GPL disclosure).
- Pricing may change and may become **dynamic**, scaling with the size of your project or
  company (for example by team size or revenue), as the project matures. The terms you buy
  under are fixed at the time of purchase.
